Trading Games Betting Board

Each round shows three groups of bets, plus bets on the board as a whole. Some prices are better than fair (+EV). Find them, size your stakes, and place your bets before time runs out. Odds are profit to stake: "pays 4:1" means a $1 stake wins $4 and you keep the $1. You start with $1,000, carried from round to round.

Categories: check 3 or more (with more than 3, a random 3 are used each round)
